FAQ

Questions

How long does an app take?

It depends on scope. A focused single-purpose app takes weeks rather than months. You get a timeline after the spec phase.

Do you publish to Google Play?

Yes. We prepare the release build, store listing, screenshots and privacy policy, and help with the submission.

Can you add AI to my app?

Yes. We build chat, photo-to-text, document questions and similar features, and can connect more than one AI model.

Do you build for iPhone and the web?

We work Android first. Web and iPhone versions can be added per project, so mention them in your brief.

Who owns the app and the code?

Ownership, payment and support terms are agreed in writing before any work starts.

How do I start?

Send a short brief using the form below. Tell us what the app does and who it is for. We reply with questions and next steps.
